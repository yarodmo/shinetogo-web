Questions, answered

Does ceramic coating make paint scratch-proof?

No. It adds a hard, slick layer that helps resist UV, bird droppings and water spots, and makes washing easier. It does not stop rock chips or deep scratches.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

It depends on the product, how the paint is prepared and how the car is washed and kept. We don’t promise a number. We confirm the product, its written warranty terms and aftercare in your quote.

How long does a ceramic coating job take?

It depends on the vehicle and the condition of the paint. The work includes washing, decontamination, polishing and curing time, so it takes longer than a wash. We confirm timing and aftercare in your quote.

What does the warranty cover?

Warranties come with the specific film or coating used, and the terms vary. Ask for the written terms for the exact product before you book.

Can I get window tint and ceramic coating together?

Yes. Ask for both in one quote. We confirm how the work is scheduled and any combined timing when we reply.

How do I get a ceramic coating quote?

Send photos of the hood, roof and sides in daylight, plus the paint color, on WhatsApp, or use the form. We reply with a price range and next steps. The final price is confirmed once we see your car.
